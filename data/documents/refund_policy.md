# Refund Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-01. Applies to all consumer orders placed on the Northwind Supply store._

## 1. Standard refund window

A customer may request a refund within **30 days of the delivery date**. Orders that have not yet
been delivered are not eligible for a refund; they are eligible for **cancellation** instead, which
is handled by the orders team.

Requests made more than 30 days after delivery are outside the standard window. They may still be
covered by a manufacturer warranty — see section 6.

## 2. Qualifying reasons

The following reasons qualify for a refund inside the standard window:

| Reason | Return required? | Evidence required? |
|---|---|---|
| Damaged on arrival | No, if the refund amount is under $50 | A photo is requested but not required |
| Defective or not working | No, if the refund amount is under $50 | A description of the fault |
| Item not as described | Yes | None |
| Wrong item shipped | Yes, prepaid label provided | None |
| Changed mind / no longer needed | Yes, customer pays return shipping | None |

Refunds of $50 or more always require the item to be returned before the refund is released, except
for damaged or defective items that the customer confirms were disposed of on our instruction.

## 3. What is refunded

Refunds cover the item price. Original shipping is refunded only when the entire order is being
refunded, or when the reason is our error — wrong item shipped, damaged on arrival, or defective.

A refund may never exceed the amount actually paid for the order.

## 4. Approval thresholds

| Refund amount | Handling |
|---|---|
| $0.00 – $50.00 | Released automatically once eligibility is confirmed |
| $50.01 – $200.00 | Requires manager approval before release |
| Above $200.00 | Escalated for manual handling by the resolutions team |

These thresholds are enforced by our systems. An agent cannot release a refund above the automatic
limit, regardless of how clear the customer's eligibility is.

## 5. One refund per order

An order that has already been refunded cannot be refunded again. If a customer believes a previous
refund was incorrect or incomplete, that is a billing dispute and is escalated for manual handling.

## 6. Warranty claims outside the window

Items with a manufacturer warranty may be replaced rather than refunded after the 30-day window.
Warranty terms are documented per product and are not covered by this policy.

## 7. Information required to act

To act on a refund request we need the **order number**. If a customer has not provided one, ask
for it. Do not attempt to guess which order a customer means, even when they have only one recent
order — acting on the wrong order creates a billing dispute that costs more to resolve than asking.
